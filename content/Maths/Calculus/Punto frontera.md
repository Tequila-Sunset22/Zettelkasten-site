> [!theorem] **Def.** (Boundary Point)
>
> Let $E$ be a set. Any point $x$ (not necessarily in $E$) is said to be a **boundary point** of $E$ provided that every interval $(x-c,x+c)$ contains at least one point of $E$ and also at least one point that does not belong to $E$.
